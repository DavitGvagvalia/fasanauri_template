# Current data and integration

Localized PWA manifests, app icons, Apple home screen metadata, and theme colors configure installed appearance. The existing SVG logo supplies the exported icons. Standalone mode depends on browser installation support. No service worker, offline menu or order cache, or background push integration is configured.

## Menu

The server loads categories through lib/menu/getCategories.ts and the complete catalog through lib/menu/getMenuCatalog.ts. Each helper simulates a 200 to 900 millisecond query. InitialMenu waits for a request below Suspense, loads both concurrently, and sends serializable records to MenuBrowser. The browser filters this complete catalog by branch, category, and multilingual search. All matching dishes are rendered on direct page requests. There is no infinite scroll.

The catalog currently comes from local JSON seeds and shared images. Zod validates domain records. Products have translated descriptions and demo prices in integer GEL minor units. Public categories, products, and product by ID HTTP routes remain available. Product dialogs can resolve direct links independently of active filters.

Switching branches on the loaded menu filters the existing catalog immediately. It does not repeat the simulated catalog query. About galleries still load on the server and show branch specific loading skeletons during navigation.

## Branches and galleries

Branch names and map links come from constants/places.ts. Contact details are withheld for every branch. Map destinations, daily hours, and photographs are temporary content. getPlaceGallery waits 200 to 900 milliseconds and returns image records with stable IDs and imageURL fields. The gallery renders the returned number of records and does not assume five images in its UI.

Branch cards and galleries share /images/placeholder_place_image.webp, served from public/images. The published WebP is a copy of the placeholder asset under assets/images.

## Cart, checkout, and legal content

The cart is browser only and persists validated selections and sample prices. Checkout validates contact fields and an optional 500 character trimmed note, snapshots items and the note in session memory, and clears the editable cart. Contact fields remain in form memory. Hydration seeds two staff pending orders, one customer confirmation request, one declined salad order with the translated reason Order was too cringe, one courier order on the way, and two delivered orders with older timestamps. The newest staff pending fixture changes to confirmation required ten seconds after hydration. It receives a sample 5 GEL delivery fee and 35 minute duration. The initial customer confirmation request has the same sample quote. Products reuse catalog seeds and the published placeholder image. Dates are relative to hydration and display in the restaurant time zone. The courier fixture uses a sample delivery fee and a fixed arrival fifteen minutes after hydration. It is a display example, not a verified payment, dispatch, or tracking event. All fixtures regenerate on reload.

The drawer groups orders under On the way, Confirmation required, Pending orders, and History, sorting each group newest first and supporting animated expansion and collapse. Details include unit prices, quantities, serving choices, line totals, food subtotal, delivery, total, duration, notes, and decline reasons. Pending delivery stays labeled pending and excluded from the provisional total. Staff acceptance simulation requires customer reconfirmation before any future payment. Confirming the sample total only updates local state.

Persistent notices always include courier records on the way, followed by customer confirmation requests, otherwise staff pending orders, otherwise confirmed records awaiting future payment. Lower priority waiting records and terminal history remain in the drawer but are hidden from the notice surface. Equal priority cards appear side by side when they fit and become a carousel only when space is insufficient. Notices omit visible order numbers. The 280 pixel notices stay to the left of any open right sidebar on desktop, reserve space for left sidebars, and hide behind full width mobile sidebars. Language, location, and order sidebars use matching motion timing. A black dot marks the Pending orders tab while customer confirmation is needed. Mobile product drawers hide the notification dock until closed. Overflow notices support touch swipes and mouse dragging with spacing between cards. The ten second transition updates the persistent notice with optional interaction unlocked sound, without a duplicate toast. Browser notifications are best effort after permission. Checkout completion asks for permission through an explicit opt in. Denied or dismissed prompts show a declined message and Ask again control, with browser specific settings steps and a directional pointer if native permission remains blocked. The help view has no top divider or external instructions link. Browser matching is local and is not retained or transmitted. Desktop permission controls sit to the left of order notices when space permits, otherwise above them. Not now lasts for the current page session. Do not show again persists a browser reminder preference, without changing native permission. Collapsing a notice only changes its in memory presentation. No background push subscription, service worker, socket, or live staff event exists. Notes and contact fields are excluded from alerts. See [browser notification limits](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Notifications_API/Using_the_Notifications_API) and [audio activation requirements](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Web_Audio_API/Best_practices).

Arrival deadlines are local fixture timestamps. Staff acceptance assigns one absolute estimatedArrivalAt based on the sample duration. The visible arrival clock uses restaurant local time and changes to Soon below one minute remaining. New checkout submissions retain an unknown deadline. None of these values are a real courier estimate.

Submitted snapshots remain independent of later cart edits, contain no contact fields, and are lost on reload along with notes. New submissions have no invented delivery quote or duration. Checkout creates no server order, sends no contact data, and processes no payment. Login does not authenticate. Terms and privacy article bodies still require actual legal content.

Checkout describes the planned manual staff review and Wolt Drive flow. The drawer shows demo order status instead of a process explanation. No staff inbox, live decline notification, delivery quote, payment, dispatch, or tracking integration exists yet. Editable cart totals cover food only. Sample delivery fees are limited to clearly labeled fixtures. New checkout totals exclude unknown delivery and state that the final amount will be shown after acceptance and before payment. No tracking link is fabricated. Staff review location, notification channels, payment provider, settlement currency, delivery coverage, and verified provider contracts remain integration prerequisites.
